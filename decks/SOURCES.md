## 3. Clinical & Health-Economic Benchmarks

*Used for CaixaImpulse deck's clinical and health-economic claims ($70k–$248k saved per patient, 30%–50% BCI illiteracy, and stroke prevalence)*

### Global Stroke Burden (~94M prevalent cases)

**Source:** GBD 2021 Stroke Collaborators, *The Lancet Neurology* (2024)
- Reports **93.8 million prevalent stroke survivors globally**
- Source Type: ✅ Peer-reviewed epidemiological study

### BCI Illiteracy Rate (30%–50%)

**Primary Sources:**
1. **Allison & Neuper (2010)**, "Could Anyone Use a BCI?" in *Brain-Computer Interfaces* (Springer)
2. **Vidaurre & Blankertz (2010)**, *Brain Topography*

**Key Findings:**
- Standard benchmarks show **15%–30% fail to achieve >70% control** with standard Motor Imagery
- **Up to 50% in clinical/stroke populations** fail to achieve reliable control
- Source Type: ✅ Peer-reviewed neuroscience literature

### Cost Savings per Patient ($70k–$248k)

**Basis for Claims:**
- Reduced hospitalization duration
- Enabled home-based rehabilitation
- Decreased need for assisted living facilities
- Reduced caregiver burden costs

**Source Type:** Health economics literature (specific citations needed for pitch materials)

## 7. Methodological Notes & Cautions

### When Citing Private Company Financials:
1. **Always specify source type** (audited filing vs. intelligence estimate)
2. **Use ranges when estimates diverge significantly** (e.g., Kinova $33M–$76M)
3. **Favor sources closer to M&A events** (e.g., Tyromotion SHS sale prep)
4. **Cross-reference multiple platforms** when possible

### Market Sizing Variations:
- **Clinical rehab hardware only:** $580M–$600M
- **Broad rehab/assistive robotics** (including surgical, elderly care): $4B–$11B
- **Always specify scope** in presentations to avoid inflated TAM claims

### Regional Considerations:
- Most data is **global market sizing**
- EU and US represent majority of addressable market
- Regulatory pathways differ (FDA vs. MDR)
- Reimbursement landscapes vary significantly
